Get a Gmail App Password

  1. Open Google Account security and turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Open App passwords, name it "PhishGuard", and press Create.
  3. Copy the 16-character password into the form.
  4. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, then Forwarding and POP/IMAP, and choose Enable IMAP.

PhishGuard reads mail without marking it as read. Your App Password is stored only in var/settings.json on this computer and is sent only to Google. You can revoke it any time from your Google account.

School or work Google accounts may block App Passwords. If so, use a personal Gmail address.
